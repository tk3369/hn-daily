Sshole: a 14-year-old musl quirk lets any long password into locked SSH accounts

A chain of small decisions — musl's crypt() returning '*' on failure, OpenSSH borrowing another account's salt for '*' accounts — means any 257–1024 character password logs into a locked account on musl systems without PAM. Alpine and OpenWrt are exposed; OpenSSH 10.6p2 fixes it.

Source HN Discuss 730 pts · 208 comments
What HN commenters are saying

The name gets an A+ from the thread, but the substance is the scope: this only bites on musl without PAM — Alpine, OpenWrt, Buildroot — and the fix is to update to OpenSSH 10.6p2 or turn password logins off entirely. Several note the bug sat in the open for a decade because OpenSSH tests on glibc and most distros route through PAM.

Insects don't swarm at LED lights, study says

A study finds insects don't swarm at LED lights the way they did at older lamps, a small win for street lighting. The thread pushes back from experience — plenty of people still see moths at their home LEDs — and asks whether flicker, not spectrum, is doing the repelling.

Source HN Discuss 298 pts · 151 comments
What HN commenters are saying

Personal experience is the main counter-evidence: commenters with LED-only homes still see moths and bees drawn to the light. The leading theory is flicker — PWM dimming and rapid flashing that insects can see and humans can't — and several note the study didn't cover high-pressure sodium lamps, the previous street-light standard.
