SpaceX catches Starship — first full recovery of an orbital rocket

Flight 15 launched Nov 13, deployed 26 Starlink V3 satellites, then both the Super Heavy booster and the ship flew back to Starbase for tower catches — the first full recovery of an orbital launch system. The flight lasted ~22 hours over ~14 orbits.

Source HN Discuss 1417 pts · 939 comments
What HN commenters are saying

Near-universal awe — “picture perfect capture” was the mood. The thread treats it as the payoff of a dozen flight tests, not a one-off. Little disagreement; the open question is how fast SpaceX turns a caught ship into a reflown one.

Lovable apps leak passports and 200K private messages

Researchers examined 159,041 Lovable-built apps: 24,664 sent Supabase records on load with no login, and 7,403 exposed email addresses. Worst cases: 80,000+ forum profiles, 200,000+ chat messages, 1,000+ records with passport numbers — usually missing row-level security.

Source HN Discuss 913 pts · 345 comments
What HN commenters are saying

Consensus: the flaw is old — missing access control — but AI builders multiply it, since the same misconfiguration pattern repeats across thousands of apps and non-technical owners can’t spot it. Disagreement: whether AI is the cause or just the accelerant; several note Railway was hit the same way days ago.

An old phone has the CPU, RAM, storage, Wi-Fi and battery a Pi project needs — for free, from a drawer. The case for repurposing instead of buying new, with the caveats: no GPIO header, and Android is harder to tame than Raspberry Pi OS.

Source HN Discuss 592 pts · 313 comments
What HN commenters are saying

Woz wrote Apple 1 BASIC on paper, by hand

Before the Apple 1 existed, Woz wrote its BASIC interpreter on paper, hand-assembling 6502 instructions, then typed it in and tested it on the machine itself. For 8-bit hobbyists with one computer, that was simply how it was done.

Source HN Discuss 257 pts · 134 comments
What HN commenters are saying

Consensus: hand-assembly on paper was the default workflow, not a stunt — slow, careful, and usually right first time. The admiration is unanimous: one commenter says it’s taken 40 years to learn a fraction of what Woz knew in 1975.
