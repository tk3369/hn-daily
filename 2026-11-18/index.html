2

Java4Shell: critical RCE in OpenJDK's Base64 decoder — patch now

Researchers disclosed CVE-2026-114938, a memory-corruption bug in OpenJDK's AVX-512-optimized Base64 decoder, rated CVSS 9.2. A remote, unauthenticated attacker can corrupt heap memory and reach code execution; the trigger is 64 'A's followed by '='. Java powers 90% of the Fortune 500, and Base64 is everywhere — cookies, JWTs, certs. Patched builds are out; a workaround is -XX:UseAVX=2.

Source HN Discuss 1068 pts · 282 comments
What HN commenters are saying

Full details were still emerging when the story broke — the write-up promised them hours after the patch. Unverified reports say only x86-64 is affected and ARM/Graviton escapes it. The recurring theme: hand-written assembly intrinsics are where 'many eyeballs' stops working, because few eyeballs understand the code.

Quad9 quits France over piracy-blocking order — a canary for the open internet

Quad9 will stop serving France on Nov 24 after a Paris court ordered it to block domains in a beIN Sports piracy case, with a €1,000/day penalty. Quad9 says it can't block for French users only without tracking them, and won't apply a French order worldwide. Cloudflare warns hospitals, schools and government offices relying on Quad9 face disruption.

Source HN Discuss 807 pts · 365 comments
What HN commenters are saying

Split between 'the balkanization of the internet is here' and 'it was never truly free — the Great Firewall, Minitel and export controls predate this by decades.' Others note Cisco's OpenDNS left France the same way, and a Belgian order now looms over Cloudflare, Google and Cisco, due January 2027.

A randomized experiment by RevScope and DataForSEO hid AI Overviews from some US desktop users: where an Overview appears, clicks to outside sites fall about 40%. Overviews now show on 61% of tracked US keywords, up from 6% two years ago, while AI assistants send sites just 0.3% of their traffic. The industry's answer, GEO, has little evidence it compensates.

Source HN Discuss 580 pts · 439 comments
